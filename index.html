(01) — About

A pre-incubation centre of BIRAC, opening its doors to the people building India’s bioeconomy — and those who back them.

The Biotech Startup Conclave and Workshop is organised by the E-YUVA Center at GIET University, a biotech pre-incubation centre of the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC), a Government of India enterprise.

For two days the campus becomes a working floor: startups exhibit what they’ve built, investors and policymakers speak plainly about what it takes, and domain mentors run hands-on sessions you can apply the following week.

(08) — FAQ

Questions, answered.

When and where is the conclave?

On 9 and 10 December 2026 at GIET University, Gunupur, Odisha, India.

Who is organising the event?

The E-YUVA Center at GIET University — a biotech pre-incubation centre of BIRAC — with support from the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC), a Government of India enterprise.

How much does a startup exhibition stall cost?

₹1,000 per person. This includes lunch and dinner, a 2 m × 2 m exhibition space, an exhibition kit and complimentary access to the International Conference (CABHBE-2026).

How do I pay the exhibition fee?

By bank or online transfer to State Bank of India, GIET University Campus branch — IFSC SBIN0017769, account name Gandhi Institute of Engineering Technology, account number 38307594449. Then enter the transaction reference (UTR) in the registration form.

Is accommodation available?

Yes. Limited on-campus accommodation is available on a first-come, first-served basis at ₹600–800 per day. Budget hotels in Gunupur cost around ₹1,000–1,200 per day; the Organizing Secretary can help you arrange one.

Whom do I contact with questions?

Call the Organizing Secretary on +91 72052 32542.
